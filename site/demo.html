    Why this is worth knowing

    None of these patterns prove anything about how a page was made, and FIG will never tell you a page “is” AI-written — detectors that claim to do that are wrong often enough to ruin someone's week. What they do show is where a page stopped making decisions. Every one of them is a choice somebody skipped, and every one is yours to take back.

    The same habits that make a page feel generic to a reader also make it thin to a model: nothing specific to quote, nothing distinctive to remember. Fixing them is the same work either way.
