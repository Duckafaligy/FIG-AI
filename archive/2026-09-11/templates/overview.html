Read your first site

Give FIG a domain and it crawls it the way a search engine does, then scores it across four layers and tells you what to change about each finding. It takes a minute or two.

Start with your own site. It is the one where you can tell whether a finding is right, which is how you learn to trust the rest.

  1. 1Add a domain — no ownership proof needed for a one-off read
  2. 2FIG checks robots.txt, finds the sitemap and reads up to 40 pages
  3. 318 checks run across Craft, Structure, Search and Answers
  4. 4Every finding gets a why and a fix, and each layer a score
  5. 5Read it again later and the dashboard shows what changed
